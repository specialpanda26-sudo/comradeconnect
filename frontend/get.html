Download for Android

Install in 3 steps

  1. Tap Download. When it finishes, tap Open on the download notice, or find comrade-connect.apk in Files > Downloads.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installs from this source. This is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  3. Open Comrade Connect and sign in.
Or use it in your browser, nothing to install

Add it to your iPhone

  1. Open comradeconnect.co.ke in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Comrade Connect from your home screen.
