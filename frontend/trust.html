If there is a problem

1. Report it from the order, before the money is released.

2. An admin reviews it within 48 hours of the report.

3. A decision: refund the buyer to their wallet, or pay the seller.

4. Not happy? Write to us for a second look. Full process: Refunds and disputes.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  • We never ask for your M-Pesa PIN, password, recovery code or handover code. Never share them with anyone, including us.
  • Listings are approved by an admin before anyone sees them.
  • Every admin action on money, disputes and accounts is written to an audit log.
  • Phone numbers, links and handles are blocked from posts and messages so nobody is pulled off the platform.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See the Privacy Policy.

Report a security problem: email us with "Security" in the subject. If anyone is in danger, call 999 or 112.
