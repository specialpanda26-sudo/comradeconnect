Refunds and disputes

Money for marketplace items is held in escrow, so the seller is not paid until the buyer is satisfied. Here is exactly how it works.

How escrow works

1. Pay. You pay on Comrade Connect. The money is held, not sent to the seller.

2. Hand over. You get a 6-digit code. The seller marks the item as handed over.

3. Confirm. Give the code to the seller only after you have checked the item. The seller is then paid.

4. Auto-release. If the seller marked it handed over and you do not confirm or dispute within the days shown on your order (3 days by default), the seller is paid.

If something goes wrong: open a dispute

  • Buyer or seller can open a dispute from the order, before payment is released. Say what went wrong in a few words.
  • Entering a wrong code 5 times also stops the order, so you can dispute it.
  • A dispute pauses auto-release. An admin reads both sides and either refunds the buyer or pays the seller.
  • The admin decision is final inside Comrade Connect. It does not remove any rights you have under Kenyan law.

Where refunds go

A refunded order is credited to the buyer's Comrade Connect wallet. Withdrawing the wallet to M-Pesa is handled by our team, and withdrawals need a verified account (see Safety).

Fees

The marketplace fee (currently 3%) is taken from the seller's payout, and is shown before a sale is confirmed. Buyers do not pay it.

Payments that were not completed

If you start a payment and do not finish it, it expires automatically and no escrow order is created. Always keep your M-Pesa message and send us the code if you paid and nothing happened.

Club items and event tickets

The refund period for Club memberships and tickets is shown before you pay.

Still stuck?

Email support@comradeconnect.co.ke with your order number and what happened. See Contact.
