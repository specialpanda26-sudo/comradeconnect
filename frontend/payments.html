Payments, escrow and payouts

Plain answers to: who holds my money, what do I pay, when does the seller get paid, and how do I get money out. Standard terms shown.

1. Who holds your money, and how

Payment method: M-Pesa sent directly to us. No automatic payment gateway is used. At checkout the app shows the M-Pesa details to pay (Send Money, Till or Paybill). The account name on the M-Pesa confirmation must read the name shown at checkout. If the name is different, do not pay.

What happens next. You enter the M-Pesa confirmation code in the app. An admin matches that code to our M-Pesa statement and confirms it. Only then is your order created and the item marked sold.

Payment gateway: IntaSend. When you pay, IntaSend sends an M-Pesa prompt to your phone. You enter your M-Pesa PIN on your phone. We never see or store your PIN. IntaSend collects the payment and passes it to us. We confirm every payment directly with IntaSend before your order is created; a message alone is never trusted.

If IntaSend cannot send a prompt, we may use another provider instead. The same rules below apply.

Payment gateway: TinyPesa. When you pay, TinyPesa sends an M-Pesa prompt to your phone. You enter your M-Pesa PIN on your phone. We never see or store your PIN. We create your order only after the payment is confirmed.

What "held in escrow" means. After your payment is confirmed, the money belongs to that one order. The seller cannot take it until a release event happens (section 3). Escrow is a record kept by comradeconnect in its own ledger. It is not a bank account, and money in escrow or in your wallet is not a bank deposit and earns no interest. The marketplace fee is worked out when the order is created and is only kept when a sale is released.

3. How an order moves, and when the seller is paid

1. Held. Your payment is confirmed. The money is held for this order.

2. Handed over. The seller marks the item as handed over.

3. Released. The seller is paid into their wallet when any one of these happens:

  • the buyer gives the 6-digit code to the seller
  • the buyer taps "I received it"
  • the buyer stays silent after "handed over" for the auto-release time
  • an admin decides after a dispute

Auto-release time: 3 days after "handed over". A dispute stops the clock.

Wrong codes. After 5 wrong codes the order stops so it can be disputed.

If something goes wrong with the payment

  • Not confirmed in time. An unconfirmed payment expires after 30 minutes. No order is created.
  • Paid, but it expired. If your money arrives after expiry, it is not lost. It goes to a person to review. If the item is still available we confirm it. If not, we return the money to your wallet.
  • Item sold to someone else first. The full amount goes to your wallet.
  • You sent too little. The payment is not accepted as a full payment. Contact us with your M-Pesa message.
  • Wrong number or wrong amount sent. Contact us with the M-Pesa code straight away.

4. Your wallet, refunds and payouts

  • Sale money and refunds are credited to your Comrade Connect wallet. You can see every entry in the wallet screen.
  • To take money out, ask for a withdrawal to your own M-Pesa number. The smallest withdrawal is KSh 50. The fee is shown before you confirm.
  • How it is paid: M-Pesa, sent by our team to the number on your withdrawal request. Each payout is recorded with its M-Pesa reference.
  • How long: we aim to pay within 24 hours of your request. This is a target, not a guarantee, and a delay for checks (below) can make it longer.
  • You can cancel a withdrawal request until it has been paid. The money goes straight back to your wallet.
  • ID check. You must be ID verified before you can withdraw. This applies to refunds too: a refund lands in your wallet, and you need to be ID verified to cash it out. See Trust centre.

5. When we may pause a payout

We may delay a release or a payout while we check for fraud, a stolen or mistaken payment, an abusive account, or a legal request. We will tell you the reason unless the law stops us, and we will not keep your money without a reason. You can dispute the delay by writing to us (see Refunds and disputes).

6. Paying outside Comrade Connect

Escrow and dispute help only cover payments made on Comrade Connect. If you pay a seller directly, or are asked to, we cannot refund you. Report it.
