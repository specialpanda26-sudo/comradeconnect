Safety and verification

Who you are talking to

  • Posts and messages are anonymous. Phone numbers, links and social handles are blocked so nobody can be pulled off the platform.
  • Marketplace listings are approved by an admin before anyone sees them.
  • You can mute, block and report any post, message or clip. Admins review reports.

ID verification

  • Comrades can ask for a verified badge by sending their role, admission or staff number and photos of their school ID.
  • Only admins see those photos, only to confirm who the person is. They are not shown to other users.
  • Withdrawing money from the wallet needs a verified account.

Payments

Payments are held in escrow until the buyer confirms. Paying outside the app removes that protection, so never do it. Read Refunds and disputes.

Age

Comrade Connect is for university and college students. Hearts is 18+ and relies on a self-declaration, not an age check.

Report a problem

Use the report button in the app, or email support@comradeconnect.co.ke. If anyone is in danger, call 999 or 112.
